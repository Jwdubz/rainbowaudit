Step 1

Found

Couples near your venue see you. Couples across town, and anyone searching for a banquet hall or a quinceanera, mostly don't.

What holds. From your corner of W Charleston Blvd you showed #2 on Google Maps for "wedding venues" and #3 or #4 for "wedding reception venues" across two checks on Nov 4, 2026. Google's AI Overview names Rainbow Gardens first for "wedding reception venues Las Vegas, NV", in 3 of 3 checks.

01

Across town, you drop out of the top 3 on Google Maps.

I ran 5 searches from 4 points in Las Vegas (your corner, Summerlin, Henderson and North Las Vegas), 20 checks in all, twice on Nov 4, 2026. You were in the top 3 in 1 of 20 on the first check and 2 of 20 on the second, and in the top 20 in 7 of 20. A paid Sponsored listing from another venue showed in 8 of 20.

Quick fix: add fresh photos and a weekly post to your Google Business Profile, and answer every new review.

02

"Banquet hall" doesn't find you at all.

For "banquet hall" you were not in the first 20 Google Maps results at any of the 4 points on Nov 4, 2026. Your own homepage title calls you a "Banquet Facility".

Quick fix: add "Banquet hall" as an extra category on your Google Business Profile, and use the words banquet hall on your homepage.

Google, Bing and your own site all list (702) 878-4646.

Solution: SEO and AI Visibility
05

The quote form asks for a field it doesn't have.

Your contact page says to write "Pricing Request" in the Subject field. The form has no Subject field. Its fields are Name, Phone, Email, Date Interested In, Number of Guest Expected and Message. Your Instagram says "Book your tour today!" and links to that same form, which has no tour option.

Quick fix: delete the Subject sentence, and add a "Book a tour" choice to the form.

06

No way to book a tour or get an answer after hours.

There is no online booking, tour scheduler or chat on the 8 pages I checked. Google lists your hours as 10 AM to 4 PM every day, and Apple matches. Your site says "Open 7 days | By Appointment" and lists no hours.

Quick fix: put the same hours on your site, and add a tour scheduler so a couple can pick a time at night or on the weekend.

Solution: Get Booked
07

Your homepage loads fast for real visitors, then jumps.

Google's own data from real phone visitors over the last 28 days shows the page loading in 1.7 seconds, which passes. The layout shift score is 0.2, and Google's pass line is 0.1, so the page fails Google's check on that one number. Of the venues I compared that have this data, Lakeside Weddings & Events passes and Rainbow Gardens doesn't.

Quick fix: give every image and banner on the homepage a fixed size so nothing moves while it loads.

Of your newest 300 Google reviews, you replied to 75 of 122 from the last 2 months, and to 1 of 178 before that. About 25 new reviews arrive each month, unevenly.
